System Title:
TSHSEMS (Taysan Senior High School Evaluation Management System)

Short Description of the System:
TSHSEMS (Taysan Senior High School Evaluation Management System) is a web-based 
platform designed to streamline the evaluation and management of students’ academic 
performance. It allows teachers to input and track grades and record daily attendance 
with automatic monthly summaries, enables students to view their performance and 
attendance history, and provides administrators with tools for reporting, class 
management, attendance monitoring, and analytics—all in a secure, efficient, and 
centralized environment.

Project Scope and Deliverables:
The Taysan Senior High School Evaluation Management System (TSHSEMS) is a web-based platform designed to digitalize and streamline the academic evaluation process of Taysan Senior High School. The scope of the system includes:
1. User Management
•	Creation and management of Admin, Teacher, and Student accounts
•	Role-based access control (Super Admin, Academic Admin, Registrar Admin, Technical Admin)
2. Student Information Management
•	Student profile creation and editing
•	Class, section, and strand assignment
•	View of student information history
3. Teacher and Class Management
•	Assignment of teachers to subjects and sections
•	Monitoring of class lists
•	Access to student profiles under their advisory or subject load
4. Grade Management
•	Input, update, and computation of student grades
•	Automated calculation of averages, remarks, and final grades
•	Secure storage and retrieval of academic records
5. Reports and Analytics
•	Generation of downloadable/printable grade reports
•	Class performance summaries and analytics
•	School-wide performance insights for administrators
•	Daily and monthly attendance summaries for teachers and administrators
6. System Administration
•	Backup and restore functionality
•	System logs and activity monitoring
•	Security features such as authentication, password encryption, and restricted access
7. Accessibility and Interface
•	Web-based system accessible on desktop and mobile
•	Responsive and user-friendly UI based on modern design standards

Define User Roles and their Permissions/Capabilities
1. ADMIN

(Combined Super Admin + Academic Admin + Registrar Admin + Technical Admin)
The most powerful and only admin type.

Permissions / Capabilities
A. User & Account Management

-Create, edit, and deactivate Teachers and Students

-Reset passwords

-Assign or reassign teachers to classes

-Manage enrollment and student sectioning

B. Academic & Grade Management

-View all submitted grades

-Validate/approve teacher-submitted grades

-Edit or correct grades if needed

-Manage subjects, sections, strands, and school year

C. System Management

-Manage system settings (school info, grading periods, announcements)

-Monitor system logs and activity

-Handle minor technical tasks (simple backups, data updates)

D. Records Management

-Access complete student profiles and academic histories

-Generate form-like documents (summary reports, list of students, etc.)

-E. Reporting & Analytics

-Generate and download all reports

-View school-wide analytics and performance dashboards

- View and generate daily and monthly attendance summaries

2. TEACHER

(For grade encoding and monitoring classes)

Permissions / Capabilities

View assigned sections and student lists

Input and update grades per subject

Submit grades for admin validation

View student profiles (read-only)

Generate summary of grades for their own classes

Receive announcements from Admin

Record daily attendance and view monthly attendance summary of their classes


3. STUDENT

(View-only, simplest role)

Permissions / Capabilities

View their own grades

View their own profile information

Download/print their grade report (optional)

Receive announcements

View their attendance record (daily and monthly summary)
